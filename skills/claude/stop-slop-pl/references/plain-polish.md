# Plain Polish Guide

Use this when the target is public-facing, instructional, civic, UX, help, or broad-audience Polish.

## Principles

- Start with what the reader needs to know or do.
- Address the reader directly when appropriate.
- Name the actor when it helps comprehension.
- Use verbs over noun-heavy constructions.
- Keep broad-audience sentences short, usually around 15 to 20 words.
- Use natural Polish order.
- Prefer concrete, familiar words.
- Explain necessary specialist terms.
- Put legal basis after the reader-facing explanation when possible.
- Keep the text correct, not artificially casual.

## Common Simplifications

| Heavy | Plain |
| --- | --- |
| `w celu` | `aby`, `żeby` |
| `celem` | `aby`, `żeby` |
| `niniejszy` | `ten` |
| `dokonać zakupu` | `kupić` |
| `dokonać zgłoszenia` | `zgłosić` |
| `ulec pogorszeniu` | `pogorszyć się` |
| `w dniu dzisiejszym` | `dzisiaj` |
| `w miesiącu maju` | `w maju` |
| `posiadać możliwość` | `może` |
| `w przypadku braku możliwości` | `jeśli nie możesz` |
| `osoba zainteresowana` | `jeśli chcesz`, `wnioskodawca` if legal role matters |

## Signals To Reduce

- passive constructions: `jest realizowane`, `zostało wykonane`,
- impersonal forms: `dokonano`, `ustalono`, `przyjęto`,
- verbal nouns: `dokonanie`, `wdrożenie`, `przeprowadzenie`,
- participles: `mając`, `dotyczący`, `obejmujący`,
- genitive chains,
- abstract nouns ending in `-ość` when they hide the action,
- repeated adjectives before nouns.

## Safety

Do not simplify away:

- deadlines,
- eligibility requirements,
- legal basis,
- required documents,
- exceptions,
- warnings,
- rights and obligations,
- steps the reader must follow.

If a legal or technical term must remain, explain it in plain words rather than replacing it with an inaccurate synonym.
